# Out of Sync — a short anime story about I

About 90 seconds. Four people in one office. Before I: tired, waiting on each
other, pulling and pushing. After I: one shared picture, AIs working side by
side, the lead watching and saying yes.

Style for every shot: modern office anime, clean line art, soft cel shading,
warm evening light through tall windows, city skyline outside, 16:9. Original
characters only. No studio, no existing show, no logos except I's own.

## The people

- **Mina** (lead). Short black hair, cardigan over a white shirt, calm, a mug
  that says "ship it". She assigns the work and says yes at the end.
- **Ravi** (backend). Hoodie, headphones round his neck, two monitors.
- **Sora** (data). Ponytail, glasses, laptop covered in stickers.
- **Jun** (front end). Rolled-up sleeves, standing desk, sketchbook.

## Part 1 — Before (grey-blue colors, slow)

1. **Office at night.** Wide shot. Four desks, monitors glowing, everyone
   slumped. A wall clock: 9:47 PM.
   *Narration:* "Four people. One product. Four different versions of it."
2. **Ravi's screen.** A red wall of merge conflicts. He rubs his eyes.
   *Ravi:* "Who changed the cart again?"
3. **Sora, headphones on,** her AI chat says "Done!" She has no idea Ravi
   renamed the function she just used.
   *Narration:* "Every AI only knew one person's half."
4. **Jun refreshes a page:** "Cart: 1 item" after two clicks. He sighs.
   *Jun:* "It worked on my machine."
5. **Mina between desks,** a sticky-note wall behind her, half the notes
   crossed out. She is asking, "Where are we?", and nobody looks up.
6. **Close-up of a chat app:** "pushed", "pull first", "wait, which branch?"
   The messages scroll faster and blur.
   *Narration:* "Push. Pull. Wait. Repeat."

## Part 2 — The switch (one beat of silence, then color comes back)

7. **Mina opens I** on her laptop. The home screen reads "Build together.
   Every AI in sync." She clicks **Start a team session**. A soft chime.
8. **Invite links fly** to three screens, like paper planes. Ravi, Sora and
   Jun each click **Join**. Their title bars switch to **I-mode**.

## Part 3 — After (warm colors, fast cuts, music lifts)

9. **Split screen of four windows.** Mina pins a decision: "Shipping is free
   over $50." It lights up in everyone's Decisions list at the same moment.
   *Narration:* "One decision. Everyone, and every AI, knows it."
10. **Ravi's AI and Sora's AI** write files at the same time. Small "in sync"
    tags pop up one after another, with no red anywhere.
    *Sora (grinning):* "It already knows Ravi renamed it."
11. **Jun asks his AI** to check the page. A small browser opens inside I,
    clicks Add to cart twice: "Cart: 1 item". The AI fixes one line, clicks
    again: "Cart: 2 items." Jun fist-bumps the air.
12. **Mina's Team activity panel:** four people, four agent runs, live steps
    ticking by. She leans back and sips from the "ship it" mug.
    *Narration:* "The lead sees everything as it happens."
13. **Mina presses Accept** on each run. Green checks. Wide shot: the clock
    says 6:12 PM, the sun is setting, and everyone is packing up, smiling.
    *Narration:* "Less waiting. More building. I — every AI in sync."
    End card: I logo, "Build together."

## What's honest to say about making it

Real anime frames need an image or video generator, or an illustrator. The
prompts above are written for that. A rough animatic, built from these
shots as title cards with the narration, can show the timing first.
