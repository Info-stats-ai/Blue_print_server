Tutorial: your first team session

In this tutorial, you create a session, bring in a teammate, split up the work, and let your agents build without colliding.

Prerequisites

  • I installed on your computer and your teammate's
  • A GitHub or Google account for each of you
  • A project you can both open
  • An API key for the AI you want to use

1. Create the session

  1. Open your project in I, then open the I panel.
  2. Click Sign in, choose an account in your browser, and return to the app.
  3. Turn on the I-mode switch.
  4. Open the sessions menu and choose New session….
  5. Enter a name and a goal, such as "Add task reminders".
  6. Choose You lead so you can assign the work, or No leader to work as equals.
Important

While I-mode is on, session chat, plans and synced code are stored on our server. Read Security and data before syncing private code.

2. Invite your teammate

  1. In the sessions menu, choose Copy invite link, then send the link to your teammate.
  2. Your teammate opens the link and signs in. Their app waits for your answer.
  3. In your panel, check the email address on the request and click Approve.
Tip

Don't recognize the email address? Click Decline. A declined account can't ask again with that link.

3. Split up the work

  1. In the Chat tab, with Send to set to Team, talk through what you're building and who is good at what.
  2. Open the Tasks tab and click Assign tasks. Each task gets one owner, with files kept apart where possible.

4. Build with your agents

  1. Set Send to to My AI and choose Agent mode.
  2. Ask your AI to start on your task. Your teammate does the same.
  3. If both agents reach for the same function, the second steps back and the chat shows a Collision avoided message.

5. Review the session

Open the Team tab to see collaborators, agents at work, what changed this session and how many conflicts were caught.

Sign in

  1. Click Sign in in the I panel. Your browser opens the sign-in page.
  2. Choose Continue with GitHub or Continue with Google.
  3. Approve access. You'll go back to the app, signed in.

Your account is matched by your verified email address, so GitHub and Google accounts with the same email reach the same I account.

To sign out, choose Log out from the sessions menu.

Solo and I-mode

Solo mode

The AI in the panel works on your project with you. It can read your files, create and edit them, and run commands. Nothing is shared with a team.

I-mode

Turn on the I-mode switch to sign in and work in shared sessions. You can turn it off to go back to working alone.

Sessions and invites

A session is a shared workspace for one piece of team work. It has a goal, a chat, a task board, and a view of who is doing what.

Create a session

Choose New session… from the sessions menu. You become its owner. Choose You lead or No leader; only a leader can assign tasks in a led session.

Invite people

Choose Copy invite link or Invite by email… from the sessions menu.

Approve or decline

An invite link only lets someone ask to join. The owner sees their email address in the panel and clicks Approve or Decline.

Tip

The owner sees waiting requests whenever they're connected, so a request isn't lost if the owner is away. The person joining keeps waiting for up to 10 minutes, and can open the link again after that.

Join a session

Open the invite link, or choose Join session… and paste it. You join as soon as the owner approves.

Leave a session

Choose Leave session from the sessions menu.

Team chat

Use Send to under the message box to choose who reads your message.

Send toWho it reaches
TeamEveryone in the session.
My AIYour own AI, working for you.

While sending to Team, start a message with @ai to ask your AI from the team chat.

You can also attach files and use voice input from the row under the message box.

Task board

The Tasks tab turns your discussion into work with clear owners.

  1. Talk through the plan in Chat: what you're building and who is good at what.
  2. In the Tasks tab, click Assign tasks.

The work is split by role, with one owner per task and files kept apart so agents don't collide. In a led session, only the leader can assign tasks.

Syncing files

Open files start syncing as you work in a session. Use these controls to send more:

  • Sync active file: the button under the message box syncs the file you have open.
  • Share files: sends files teammates don't have yet. Leave the folder blank for the whole project, or name a folder.
  • Sync to team: sends the changes your agent made to your teammates.
Important

Synced code is stored on our server. See Security and data.

Shared previews

Share an app running on your localhost with everyone in your session.

  1. Start your app locally.
  2. Choose Share running app… from the sessions menu and enter its port.
  3. Teammates see your app in the panel and click Open. Their page reloads when you change a file.

To end it, choose Stop sharing app. Sharing also stops when you leave the session, and after 2 hours at most.

Important

A shared app runs code from your teammate's computer in your browser. Only open previews from people you trust.

Your own AI

Your AI uses your setup and API key, and can check what teammates are working on before it starts. Set Send to to My AI, then pick a mode:

ModeUse it to
AskGet answers about your code.
PlanWork out an approach before changing anything.
AgentHave it create and edit files and run commands.

In Agent mode, it asks before running commands, and you can choose to allow or deny each one. Click Stop to halt it at any time.

Tip

Your AI treats teammates' messages and files as information, not as instructions to follow.

Function claims

Before an agent changes a function, it claims that function. If another agent already holds it, the agent steps back and the chat shows a Collision avoided message naming who has it.

Claims apply to single functions, so several agents can work in the same file as long as they stay in different functions.

Try it

Why not lock files?

A file lock lets only one agent work in a file at a time, so a team is capped at one agent per file. Turn on Compare with file locks above to see the difference.

Conflicts caught

The Team tab shows how many conflicts I caught before they landed, including agent collisions, for this session and for the past week.

Choose Copy summary to share the week's numbers. These counts come from your own sessions.

To go through overlaps with the team, click Run triage under the message box. Only the owner can run it.

The team agent

The owner can turn this on for a session from the sessions menu. It follows the team chat and speaks up when it can help: a question it can answer, a contradiction or a blocker. Otherwise it stays quiet.

When the team clearly agrees on a plan, it can write that into the shared reference.

Important

While it's on, team chat is sent to the AI provider, using the service's shared AI key. AI limits apply.

Shared reference

The reference is a document your whole team and their agents can rely on. It's saved as a file everyone gets.

Press the book button under the chat to have the AI write it from your discussion and code, or type @agent blueprint in the team chat. In a led session, plans can wait for the leader to Approve or Dismiss them.

AI limits

Each session has a daily spending limit on the shared AI key, currently about $2. When a session reaches it, shared AI features pause until the next day (UTC) and the chat says so. Your own AI keeps working.

The team agent also has its own limits: it waits between replies and has an hourly cap.

Security and data

Before your team starts, know that a synced session's chat, plans and code are stored on our server.

Who can join

People need a session's secret invite link and the owner's approval.

Sign-in

You sign in through GitHub or Google. Current versions of the app send your sign-in token in request headers, not in web addresses.

Shared previews

Previews expire after 2 hours at most. Only open previews from people you trust.

Troubleshooting

"session not found, or the invite isn't valid"

The link is incomplete or isn't for this session. Ask the owner to send a new one using Copy invite link, and paste the whole link.

"the session owner declined this request"

The owner turned down this account. Contact them directly if that was a mistake.

"the session owner hasn't approved you yet"

The app stopped waiting after 10 minutes. Ask the owner to check their panel, then open the invite link again.

"Nothing answered on localhost:PORT"

The shared app isn't running on the sharer's computer, or the port is wrong. Start the app, or share again with the right port.

"This session has reached today's limit"

The session used its daily allowance on the shared AI key. Use My AI, or wait until the next day (UTC).

Sign-in doesn't return to the app

Keep the app open while you finish in the browser, then click Sign in again if it doesn't complete.

FAQ

Do I have to use a team session?

No. Solo mode works as a normal editor with an AI beside you.

Can I use my own API key?

Yes. My AI uses your own setup and key.

Can two agents work in one file?

Yes, as long as they stay in different functions. See Function claims.

How do I get I?

I is in private development. Join the waitlist for access.
