Sign in

  1. Click Sign in in the app. Your browser opens the sign-in page.
  2. Choose Continue with GitHub or Continue with Google.
  3. Approve access. You'll go back to the app, signed in.

Your account is matched by your verified email address, so GitHub and Google accounts with the same email reach the same I account.

Solo and sync

In solo mode, collaboration is off and I works as a normal editor. Turn on sync to work with your team in a shared session.

Sessions and invites

A session is a shared workspace for one piece of team work. It has a goal, a chat, and a list of who is doing what.

Start a session

Create a session from the I panel and give it a name and goal. You become its owner.

Invite your team

Copy the invite link from the session menu, or invite someone by email. Anyone with the link can ask to join after signing in. You see their email address in the panel and choose Approve or Decline. They join as soon as you approve.

Function claims

Before an agent changes a function, it claims that function. If another agent already holds the claim, the request is refused and the agent is told who holds it.

Claims apply to single functions, so several agents can work in the same file as long as they stay in different functions.

Conflicts

The conflicts view shows where people and agents overlap while the work is still in progress, so you can sort it out before anyone has to merge.

Shared previews

Share an app running on your localhost with everyone in your session.

  1. Start your app locally.
  2. Open the session menu, choose to share your running app, and enter its port.
  3. Teammates get a private link. The page reloads for them when you change a file.

Sharing stops when you stop it or leave the session, and after 2 hours at most. Only open shared links from people you trust.

Your own AI

Type @agent in the team chat to send a request to your own AI, using your own setup and API key. Your AI can also check what teammates are working on before it starts.

The team agent

When it's turned on for a session, the team agent follows the chat. It answers when it can help, flags ownership clashes, and records agreed decisions in the session's shared reference.

It runs on the server and uses the service's AI key. What your team writes in the chat is sent to the AI provider.

AI limits

Each session has a daily spending limit on the shared AI key, currently about $2. When a session reaches it, shared AI features pause until the next day (UTC). Your own AI keeps working.

Security and data

Before your team starts, know that a synced session's chat, plans and code are stored on our server.

  • Joining: people need a session's secret invite link and the owner's approval.
  • Sign-in tokens: current versions of the app send tokens in request headers, not in web addresses.
  • Your code and chat: when sync is on, session chat, plans and synced code are stored on our server so teammates can receive them. In solo mode, sync is off.
  • AI providers: text sent to shared AI features, including team chat when the team agent is on, goes to the AI provider.
