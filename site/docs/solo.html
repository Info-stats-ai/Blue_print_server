Solo: you and your AI

Solo is I-IDE on its own: an editor with your AI built in. Nothing is shared with anyone.

Start

  1. On the home screen, select Open a project under Solo, and pick your project folder.
  2. Add your AI once: see Set up your AI.

Your AI

Your AI is in the Agent panel. It can read your files, create and change them, and run commands, and it asks before anything risky. Answers appear as they're written.

Switch to I-mode

Select Solo at the right of the title bar and choose I-mode to work with your team. Your AI keeps what you told it. See What I-mode does.
