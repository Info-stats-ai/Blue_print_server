Team chat

The Chat tab is where you talk to your team and your AI. The Send to control decides who reads each message.

Choose who gets your message

Send toWho reads it
TeamEveryone in the session.
My AIYour own AI. It's private to you.

Send a message to your team

  1. Set Send to to Team.
  2. Type in the message box.
  3. Press Return.

Ask your AI from team chat

While sending to Team, you can reach your AI without switching:

  • Start a message with @ai to ask your AI.
  • End a request with @agent to hand it to your AI, which can change files. Send to switches to My AI for your next messages. Switch back to Team to talk to people only.
