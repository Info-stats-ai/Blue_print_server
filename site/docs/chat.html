Team chat

The Chat tab is where you talk to your team and your AI. The Send to control decides who reads each message.

Choose who gets your message

Send toWho reads it
TeamEveryone in the session.
My AIYour own AI. It's private to you.

Send a message to your team

  1. Set Send to to Team.
  2. Type in the message box.
  3. Press Return.

Team chat is for people

Messages sent to Team go to the people in the session. To ask your AI, switch Send to to My AI, or use the Agent Panel on the left. The two work as a pair; see Your two AIs.

When the team has agreed what to do, end a message with @agent to hand the agreed work to your AI. Send to then switches to My AI.
