The I-IDE interface

The I-IDE window has an editor in the middle, panels on each side, and a status bar along the bottom. This page explains each part.

Main areas

  • Project Panel: your project's files and folders, on the left. Toggle the left side with Cmd+B.
  • Editor: where you read and change code. Each open file gets a tab.
  • I-mode panel: sign-in, team sessions, chat and your AI, on the right. Toggle the right side with Cmd+Alt+B.
  • Bottom dock: the terminal and other tools. Toggle it with Cmd+J.
  • Status bar: buttons for panels, your cursor position and the current language.

Arrange the window

Select the layout button at the right of the title bar to show or hide each side, the bottom panel and the status bar, and to choose where the terminal goes.

  • Two panels on one side: opening a panel on a side that already shows one keeps both, one above the other. For example, open Git and it appears below I-mode, or open the Agent Panel and it appears below your files. Drag the line between them to share the space, and select a panel's status bar button again to hide just that panel.
  • Terminal beside your code: the terminal opens at the bottom. To have it at a side instead, choose Beside the editor, right or left, so the side panels stay in view.
  • Resize parts of I-mode: drag the line under the session title, or the line above the message box, to make that part taller or shorter. Double-click a line to put it back.

To have each panel replace the one on its side instead, add "stack_side_panels": false to your settings.

The I-mode panel

From top to bottom, the panel has these parts during a session:

  1. Header: the words I-mode, how many people are present, the people menu and the I-mode switch.
  2. Session title: the session name and goal, with a button that copies the invite link.
  3. Tabs: Chat, Tasks and Team.
  4. Send to: choose whether your message goes to your Team or My AI.
  5. Tool row: effort level and the buttons described in Team chat.
  6. Message box: where you type.
  7. Mode and model: Ask, Plan or Agent, and which AI model to use.

The people menu

Select the people icon next to the I-mode switch to invite people, share files or your running app, and leave a session. If you're not in a session, it's where you create or join one.

The Command Palette

Press Cmd+Shift+P and start typing what you want to do. The palette lists matching commands with their shortcuts, which makes it the fastest way to learn the app.
