Sign in

You need an account to use I-mode. You sign in with GitHub or Google, and you don't need a separate password.

Sign in

  1. Turn on the I-mode switch in the I panel.
  2. Select Sign in. Your browser opens the sign-in page.
  3. Choose Continue with GitHub or Continue with Google.
  4. Approve access in the provider's window.
  5. Go back to I. The panel says Logged in.

Keep I open while you finish in your browser, because the app waits there for your sign-in to complete.

How accounts work

Your account is matched by your verified email address. If your GitHub and Google accounts use the same email, both reach the same I account.

Sign out

Select the people icon, then Log out.
