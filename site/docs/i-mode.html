What I-mode does

I-mode turns the editor into a shared workspace. This page explains the ideas you'll meet in the rest of these docs.

Solo mode and I-mode

I-IDE starts in solo mode. The AI in the panel can read your files, create and edit them, and run commands, and nothing is shared with a team.

Turn on the switch in the top-right corner of the I-mode panel to use I-mode, which adds sign-in, sessions and team chat. You can switch back at any time. Your choice is saved as the collab_mode setting.

Key ideas

TermMeaning
SessionA shared workspace for one piece of work, with a goal, chat, task board and synced files.
OwnerThe person who created the session. Only they can approve people, run triage and turn on the team agent.
LeaderIn a You lead session, the person who controls file sharing and assigns tasks.
Your AIThe AI you set up, working for you with your key. See Your AI.
Team agentAn optional agent on the server that follows team chat. See The team agent.
ClaimAn agent's reservation on a function while it changes it. See Function claims.
ReferenceA REFERENCE.md file that records the team's plan. See Shared reference.

What stays private

Messages sent to My AI aren't posted to the team chat. Your AI can look up what the team is doing when it needs to, and your teammates see the work it syncs.

Important

What you sync goes through our server. See Security and data.
