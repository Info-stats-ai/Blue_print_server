Task board

The Tasks tab turns your team's discussion into a plan, with one owner for each task.

Create a plan

  1. In Chat, talk through what you're building and who is good at what. The more specific you are, the better the split.
  2. Open the Tasks tab.
  3. Select Assign tasks.

I-IDE splits the work by role, with one owner per task and files kept apart so that agents don't collide. In a You lead session, only the leader can do this.

Stay in your lane

If someone edits files outside their task, the session counts a lane warning, which appears in the Team tab. It's a prompt to check in with each other, and nothing is blocked.

Important

Assigning tasks uses the service's shared AI key, so AI limits apply.
