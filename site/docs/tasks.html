Tasks

Tasks come from a plan. Plan with your AI, share the plan, and its steps become the team's tasks, each with an owner.

Create tasks from a plan

  1. In the I-mode panel, choose Plan in the mode menu and describe the work.
  2. Answer your AI's questions, then check the plan. Each step names an owner, taken from the spec or the chat, or whoever is best placed.
  3. Select Share with team. The plan goes to the team chat and its steps become tasks.

In a You lead session, the leader's share creates the tasks; anyone else's plan goes to the chat for the leader to use.

See and work on tasks

Tasks are in the Team activity panel beside your files, under the list of who's here and what each person is editing.

Each task shows its state in color: grey for to do, blue for doing, green for done, and its owner's color.

Stay in your lane

If someone edits files outside their task, the session counts a lane warning in Team activity. It's a prompt to check in with each other, and nothing is blocked.

Important

Turning a plan into tasks uses the service's shared AI key, so AI limits apply.
