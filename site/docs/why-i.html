Why teams use I

When everyone has their own AI, the AIs don't talk to each other. Work gets done twice, or undone. I-IDE fixes that.

Three problems, gone

  • Every AI knows the plan. Agree on something once and pin it. From then on, everyone's AI follows it. No more repeating yourself to each one.
  • Nobody breaks anybody's work. Change something a teammate's code depends on and they're told right away, before it causes trouble. One click and their AI fixes it.
  • No merge mess. Work on the same files at the same time. Changes combine on their own, and every version is kept, so you can always go back.

Work alone or together

You pick when you open the app, and one switch changes it later.

  • Solo: just you and your AI. Works like the code editor you already know, with an AI that can read, write and run your code. Nothing is shared.
  • I-mode: you, your team and everyone's AI. One live session. Code, chat and each AI's work stay in step, so the team moves as one.

See What I-mode does for more.

You stay in control

Sharing with a team shouldn't mean giving things up.

  • You choose what's shared. Send changes automatically or one file at a time. Passwords, keys and private folders never leave your computer.
  • AI work gets checked. Accept an AI's work or send it back. If something is wrong, flag it, and every AI on the team stops using it. See Agent runs.
  • Your own AI account. Use your own Claude or OpenAI key. You see what each task used. See Set up your AI.
