One editor, solo or with the whole team

Work alone with collaboration turned off. Sync when you want your team, and their agents, in the same session.

Claims by function

Agents reserve the function they're changing, so two of them can work in one file without overwriting each other.

Conflicts in one view

A dashboard shows where people and agents overlap while the work is still in progress.

A team agent that listens

Turn it on and it follows the team chat, flags ownership clashes, and records what you agreed in a shared reference.

Your own AI knows the team

Mention your agent in chat, and it can check what your teammates are working on before it starts.

Shared previews

Give teammates a private link to the app on your localhost. It reloads for them when you change a file.

Clear division of work

A shared goal and task board show who picked up which part of the job.

More agents can work at once

File locks cap a team at one agent per file. In our simulation of 4 files with 10 functions each, function-level claims kept far more agents working as the team grew.
